# BESS project comparison standard

## RAG rubric

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | Supported and materially aligned; no action beyond normal confirmation. |
| Amber | Plausible but definition, evidence, vintage, or sensitivity needs clarification. |
| Amber-Red | Material value or bankability uncertainty; close before final underwriting. |
| Red | Missing, internally inconsistent, untraceable, or capable of changing value/structure; close before reliance. |

## Comparison categories

### Project and transaction perimeter

- Project identity, jurisdiction, sponsor, asset-owning vehicle, ownership and cap table
- Development rights, land rights, grid rights, permits, exclusivity and transferability
- Transaction structure, securities, price, development payments, earn-outs and conditions precedent
- Selected model case, valuation date, COD, life, repowering/augmentation and exit

### Market and revenue

- Wholesale, day-ahead, intraday, balancing and imbalance definitions
- Executable versus theoretical revenue and capture rate
- Route-to-market scope, optimiser fee, performance benchmark and termination rights
- Ancillary/system-service products, qualification, saturation and opportunity cost
- Capacity/capability mechanisms: award versus forecast, de-rating, tenor, milestones and penalties
- Toll, floor, swap, PPA or other contracted structure: price, tenor, indexation, availability and credit
- Revenue stacking, exclusivity, dispatch priority, SoC reservation and double-count protection
- Market buildout, cannibalisation, constraint/curtailment and skip-rate assumptions
- Source vendor, vintage, licence, unit, gross/net and real/nominal basis

### Technical-to-market bridge

- Nameplate, POI, BOL/EOL usable energy and duration
- RTE, auxiliary consumption, availability, cycling and degradation
- Augmentation/repowering, warranty throughput, operating temperature and environmental derating
- Dispatch restrictions, SoC floor/ceiling, maintenance and outages
- Degradation or loss assumptions embedded in vendor curves versus applied again in the model

### Grid and charging

- Connection offer, voltage, capacity, energisation date and milestone security
- Firm/non-firm rights, import/export limits, constraints, losses and curtailment compensation
- TNUoS, DUoS, BSUoS or local equivalents; import/export and embedded treatment
- Grid works, contestable/non-contestable scope, reinforcement cost and delay risk

### Cost and financing

- Complete sources and uses; binding EPC/BESS/grid quotes; contingency and escalation
- O&M, LTSA, insurance, land, rates/tax, market fees, augmentation, decommissioning and reserves
- Contracted versus merchant cash-flow classification
- Debt sizing, DSCR, gearing cap, tenor, amortisation, balloon, covenants and hedging
- Discount rates, inflation, tax, VAT/GST, working capital and distributions
- Base, downside, severe downside and contract-failure cases

### Equity diligence beyond the dashboard

- Corporate authority, title, licences, litigation and related-party arrangements
- Counterparty credit, guarantees, collateral, termination payments and lender step-in
- Construction programme, interfaces, LDs, performance guarantees and acceptance tests
- Insurance programme and uninsurable exposures
- HSE, fire safety, environmental, biodiversity, community and supply-chain matters
- Data rights, cyber security, operational controls and reporting
- Exit route, buyer universe, transfer restrictions, residual value and decommissioning liability

## Variance classification

Classify each difference before judging it:

- **Fact:** one source is outdated or wrong.
- **Scope:** asset size, duration, geography or revenue stack differs.
- **Definition:** gross/net, theoretical/realised, nameplate/de-rated, BOL/EOL or real/nominal differs.
- **Timing:** observation period, delivery year, COD or contract term differs.
- **Case:** base/downside/upside or selected scenario differs.
- **Evidence:** assumption may be reasonable but lacks the supporting document.

## Investment impact test

Mark a gap material when it can change any of:

- annual revenue or operating cost by 5% or more;
- contracted share or debt capacity;
- COD, operating life or exit year;
- capex, contingency or required equity;
- warranty compliance or usable duration;
- ownership/control of revenue rights;
- termination/default exposure; or
- the ability to obtain permits, connect, operate, finance or sell the asset.
