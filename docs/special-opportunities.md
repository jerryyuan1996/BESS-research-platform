# Special opportunities in the investment platform

Research cut: 27 October 2026. Implementation: 28 October 2026.

The home page gives direct investment strategies for NEM Sub-5, ERCOT small
distribution batteries and German existing connections. The other registered
markets have their own special route; the eight scout markets remain research
links. Recommendations carry an execution strategy, delivery target and, for
German connection acquisitions, an explicit rights-price and build budget.
The supporting assumptions are expandable.

The German recommendation is a required acquisition mandate, not an assertion
that connection reuse automatically reduces all construction costs by 30–40%.
The default screen requires a 36-month conventional route versus 18 months,
and a total remaining construction budget 30% below the conventional reference
(40% for the developer lens). This includes retrofit works. If priced works
cannot deliver that budget, the buyer must rerun the model before bidding.
The displayed home purchase ceilings round down.

Both designs are evaluated through the existing acquisition cash-flow engine:
the same entry stage, nominal local currency, project tax convention, operating
life, investor target, all-cash pre-operation funding, and hold/sale policy.
The conventional play's actual route, duration and grant cohort are retained.
Small alternatives use their actual MW and merchant route, with no inherited
large-project award or automatic grant. Unit construction/operating uplifts,
retrofit budgets, avoided works and at-risk revenue deductions are explicit.
Development success/delay/failure probabilities are held equal.

The maximum additional rights consideration is the lower of:

1. The special project's acquisition ceiling at the common target, less the
   common purchase price.
2. The additional price at which neither the hold NPV nor the sale NPV falls
   below the corresponding conventional project's NPV.

No positive premium is reported when either test fails. This compares economic
value; it does not assert that all IRR measures rise when COD moves earlier.
The table also shows the actual return and binding ownership route.

Optional revenue-qualification delays reduce each annual revenue bucket for the
unqualified fraction while costs continue. Expiry stays anchored to COD.
The engine books annual receipts at operating year end. It does not simulate
hourly dispatch, product-specific qualification or all calendar-vintage award
conditions; those require the project model and executed contract.

The home projection is calculated at build time by the same model. The full
interactive comparison loads only on market detail or when opened in the
Investment Lab. No project price is written to URLs or sent to a service.
Users can download assumptions and dated cash flows for review.

Market overview directly reuses house-view and orientation blocks from the
existing full research dashboard, including the Japan regional lens. Revenue
economics explains payer, system problem, price formation, long-term drivers
and investment implications, with primary-source links. The cash-flow/debt
chart is removed from overview; the revenue waterfall remains.
