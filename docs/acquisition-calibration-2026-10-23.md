## Method and review boundaries

- Currency base proxies remain dated house references, not live government-bond quotes. Revised reference risk premiums are calibrated conventions; an explicit user target takes priority.
- The 15/20/25-year windows include contract expiry, fade and service costs. The 25-year case is conditional, not the default life of every battery.
- Generic straight-line depreciation, loss offsets and interest relief require replacement with local project tax details when available.
- Finland hourly-reserve increments and negotiated contract prices are stated assumptions, not observed offtaker quotations.
- The NEM CIS example corrects the collar arithmetic; private floor, ceiling and caps remain illustrative.
- US investment credits are a separate optional eligibility scenario. They are absent from the table above.
- A negative result stays visible as an achievable construction/income condition. The release does not target a positive valuation in every market.
