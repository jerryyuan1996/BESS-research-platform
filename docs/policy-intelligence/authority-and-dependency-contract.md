# Policy authority and dependency contract

| Domain | Policy process reads | Policy process writes | Publication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified policy facts | Approved official metadata and source excerpts | Candidate facts | Independent evidence validator, then policy-only writer after activation |
| Policy analysis | Same canonical claims | Untrusted hypotheses/scenarios | Routine policy analysis only; material investment recommendation split to review |
| Approved investment view | Public IDs/approved text/date | Nothing | Existing independently preapproved `rule` path unchanged |
| Methodology/assumptions | Traceable public references | Nothing | Analyst-owned; no policy trigger, writer or author inheritance |

Do not modify `AUTHOR_RIGHTS`, `LIVE_AUTHORS`, `SCOUT_LIVE_AUTHORS`, existing source lists, thresholds, scoring or discount rates. No policy module imports the View Writer, rules or investment model. An allowed write is not a permission to publish unverified facts.

Canonical records: PolicyInstrument (stable jurisdiction/official ID); PolicyEvent (announcement, issue, adoption, effect, suspension, withdrawal, repeal, correction); EvidenceSource (official URL, locator, original language, body hash, rights, checked-at); PolicyClaim (kind, status, evidence IDs and bilingual wording); ImpactDatum (observed/attributed forecast/transparent scenario, unit/currency/denominator/period/formula); PolicyStory (same claim graph across reading modes); PolicySnapshot (schema version, content digest, parent, immutable history); dependency link and ReviewCase. Every identity and reference validates; legal effect is never inferred from a news headline or announcement. Date fields remain distinct and may be unknown.

Dependency link: policy event/claim ID → canonical investment market ID → explicit public view/story/assumption reference, typed relation, mechanism, source evidence, relevance, last-approved date and review state. Unmapped links stay diagnostic; do not invent a thesis/claim correspondence. A resolver receives read-only public registries, verifies existence and returns a copy. It does not load private files or write triggers.

Materiality outcomes: `routine`, `material_investment_review`, `uncertain_or_conflicting`, `not_applicable`. Binary BESS qualification, connection access/cost, revenue entitlement, legal deadline and potential investment-call reversal can trigger review without an IRR. Do not reuse the model's numeric thresholds as policy thresholds. Unsupported citations or conflicting source evidence quarantine regardless of economic size.

Example synthetic review queue: a verified eligibility change for market `gb` references an existing public verdict. Sidecar says pending reassessment and cites that event; approved verdict/text remains unchanged. Review packet contains the approved value, proposed interpretation, evidence, uncertainty and analyst action. A legal-state update may publish after verification independently of the pending investment interpretation, once policy publication is authorized.

Lifecycle branches and append-only corrections preserve former published statements at stable version URLs. Wrong dates, draft-as-law, fake numbers, missing original locators, unsupported translations and restricted rights fail closed. Evidence strength and materiality are different dimensions. NEM and WA applicability is explicit, never inherited from an Australia news tag.
