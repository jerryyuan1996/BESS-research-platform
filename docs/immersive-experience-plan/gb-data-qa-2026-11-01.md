## Registration and calculation

`gb-dynamic-response` is the fixed 2020–2023 vintage. `gb-eac-response` is the live response-results dataset, initially loading FY2023–FY2025 and the current file. Both cite the dataset-specific [NESO licence](https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/neso-open-licence) with **Supported by National Energy SO Open Data**. Neither bears on a financial input.

The connector selects only the six approved Dynamic products. Summary observations carry the service, delivery period, availability clearing price and cleared MW. By-unit queries return totals grouped by technology and delivery period, with no participant, company, unit, address or postcode. Only case-insensitive `Battery` and `Batteries` count as batteries. All remaining labels, including blank and `NA`, stay other or unlabelled. Cancelled historic results are excluded; an unknown cancellation flag blocks the import. Any mismatch against summary MW withholds the technology layer instead of inventing its share.

The monthly price is `sum(price × cleared MW) / sum(cleared MW)`. A zero denominator stays missing. Lower-panel MW is the mean per EFA block, never a sum of maxima. The early daily masterdata uses accepted-bid fees, weighted by accepted MW, represents six EFA blocks per procurement day and remains a separately labelled method. It stops before the first later DCL summary observation, preventing overlap. London wall-time historic results are converted to UTC; already-UTC masterdata and EAC instants retain their clock. Months use the local EFA day. Source SQL and resource identifiers travel with every snapshot.

Initial source QA: 23,773 fixed-vintage records (349 early procurement periods and 23,424 later service blocks), plus 39,420 EAC response blocks. EAC origins are FY2023 5,400, FY2024 13,140, FY2025 13,140 and the current file 7,740. Every technology layer reconciles. Fixed-vintage 2023 volume-weighted prices reproduce CALC-GB-PRICE: DCL 3.0555, DCH 3.8307, DRL 9.9562, DRH 1.6727, DML 4.0095 and DMH 3.2031 GBP/MW/h. These are QA calculations, never prose constants in the story. The first masterdata observation is already after launch: no missing first day is invented.

The browser will request the monthly summaries only after the story opens. It will break lines at the auction-platform/method boundaries, preserve negative prices and provide the same monthly table. It will show the approved unscaled Example whenever validated summaries are unavailable. No offered-MW series is registered: sell orders have not been imported. No requirement line, annualised income, trading spread or financial result is derived.

## Six-hour job behaviour

There is no workflow or schedule change. On a cold start the connector loads the fixed vintage and the three EAC archives. Once present, the fixed vintage skips before network access. EAC archives already in the provenance ledger are retained; only the current summary and technology totals are queried on the existing six-hour run. A newly available financial-year archive is added once. API pagination is bounded and ordered; a failed read retains the previous snapshot under the existing runner policy. Source or fetch timestamps alone do not change the content checksum, so identical results create no six-hour data commit. The two new datasets can add their full snapshot and monthly summary to the usual `public/data/` commits when observations change; they create no drift proposal.
