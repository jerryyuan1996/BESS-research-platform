## Reading the verification labels

**Verified foundation** means the specific statement below was supported by inspected repository content or an official page/excerpt. It does not certify every statement on that website, all historical facts, a dataset's redistribution rights, or any project revenue. **Discovery only** means a source owner or document has been identified for later work. **Proposed interpretation** means an editorial or investment connection requiring review. All checks in this register are bounded to planning needs.

| Question | What does a typical summer business day ask of the SWIS, from the midday trough to the evening peak, and where does the seven-hour window sit on it? |

| Why this series | AEMO's ESOO reports unscheduled operational demand because it leaves out large-scale storage charging (SRC-WA-08, §1.6.1), which would otherwise fill in the midday trough the story explains. | Method | Mean of the six five-minute values in each 30-minute Trading Interval, labelled by its start time as the ESM Rules label Trading Intervals; then, for each of the 48 intervals, the median across the 82 days. | Licence and attribution | AEMO copyright permissions (SRC-WA-13). Caption: "Source: AEMO WEM data, Hot Season 2025-26, median business day" |
| Limits | Not a forecast, not 2028-29 demand, and not the stress day AEMO uses to set the requirement. The window it is drawn against comes from SRC-WA-08, not from this trace. Refresh after each Hot Season, keeping the earlier vintage |
