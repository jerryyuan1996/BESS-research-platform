## 1. A shared causal structure with different market stories

All markets use six chapters, but a template must not reduce every story to renewable growth followed by battery profits.

| Chapter ID | Investor's question | Required causal link | Reviewable output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `origins` | Why did this system develop here? | Geography, resources, settlement, industry and system/interconnection boundary | Labelled map; one geographic investment implication |
| `choices` | Why were these assets and rules chosen? | Constraint at the time -> decision -> inherited infrastructure/institution -> present consequence | 4-6 historical nodes, with evidence and separate causal interpretation |
| `tensions` | What is difficult today? | Distinguish total energy, timing, network location and rapid system response | One dominant tension, a secondary complication, and auditable 2D evidence |
| `battery` | What can a battery change? | Service -> power/energy/SOC/location/availability limits -> competing resource | Controlled with/without demonstration and station cutaway |
| `payment` | Who pays, on what conditions? | Service -> buyer/market -> qualification/award -> performance -> settlement | Payment path with project evidence status and incompatible obligations |
| `investment` | Why is the call what it is, and what could weaken it? | Call and play -> what could weaken them -> links into the market page's Bankable case, Revenue, Execution and Downside | One value-weakening beat and the links; no second financial view |

Every story argues for the market's published call and play (D1). Start from the market's thesis in the briefing ([REPO-12](source-register.md#repository-verification)) and write the causal chain that leads to it. The draft must survive three challenges: “Does it lead to this market's call and play?”, “Could the same opening describe any other market?” and “What evidence would show it is wrong or incomplete?” If any answer is weak, revise before drawing anything.

## 2. Story-card standard and completion states

Each short card should fit approximately 250-400 words after research. It must contain the fields below, even if an unresolved field is explicitly marked pending.

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and boundary | Allocation ID, market name, physical system/zone, shared research owner and applicable rule period |
| Call anchor | The market's published call, play and thesis, referenced from the briefing model rather than copied, with the version the card was checked against |
| One-sentence distinction | A local causal argument in plain language that leads to the call; no figures typed in |
| Historical path | 4-6 candidate nodes; verified events separately identified from pending dates and inferred causes |
| Present tension | Main problem plus a secondary constraint; how energy/timing/location/response differ |
| Battery contribution and limit | A specific service, physical boundary and at least one alternative resource |
| Payment and investability | Buyer/market, access/award/contract/performance gates, model versus project evidence |
| Value weakening | Competition, network change, new flexibility, demand shift, rule change or contract expiry |
| Evidence, uncertainty and next action | Claim/source IDs, missing datasets/clauses, reviewer status and the next diligence question |

All 18 entries below are **proposed theme cards, editorial status `proposed`**. Before drafting a card, read the market's thesis in the briefing. Where the card and the thesis disagree, the thesis wins unless the analyst changes it through the briefing process. In v1.1 the WA, NEM, Germany and Finland rows were rebuilt this way. In v1.3 the other 14 were rebuilt against the view store and the briefing shortlist on `main` at `bd62af1` (31 October 2026); each opens with the infrastructure-fund call, route and play ID it leads to. Ontario, Saudi Arabia and Spain have no view yet, so their cards lead to the thesis in their comparison module until the analyst publishes a call. The canonical allocation IDs were verified in the repository; most story claims have not been refreshed in this task. A few narrow pilot foundations are separately recorded as verified in [the source register](source-register.md#verified-claim-seeds). A market's existing model readiness is not story-card completion.

Priority means production order: **P0** WA complete pilot; **P1** DE/FI distinct-story validation; **P2** Italy mechanism validation; **P3** reusable extension candidates. Within P3, the suggested cohorts can change after measured pilot effort. They are not investment rankings or commitments to concurrent production.
