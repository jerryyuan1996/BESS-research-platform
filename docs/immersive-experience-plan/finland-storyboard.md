## 1. Scope, argument and reading route

**Explain the current fund call: a selective investment in trading and reserves under a negotiated floor.** Finland shares Nordic frequency and buys clearly defined response services. Storage has already overtaken the gross scale of its fast-reserve obligations, so reserve need alone cannot carry the investment case. Trading carries the modelled revenue; the floor is what makes part of it secured, for its stated tenor. The duration comes from the current play and model, not a claim that reserve products universally require that duration.

Allocation: `fi`; physical boundary: mainland Finland within the Nordic synchronous area. Default lens: `infraFund`. `reviewedAgainst`: **REPO-FI-15**, commit `1fbdff5995ef68b9ddd0309f6ae4f98778e57fb9`, evaluated for 2026-10-31; verdict `opportunistic`, displayed **Situational / 择机投入**, tier `situational`, play **`fi-toll-2h`**, route `toll`, entry `ready-to-build`, evidence `fair`. **This differs from v1.1's `fi-mer-2h`**. Trading plus a floor is now the argument, rather than a reserve-led merchant play. The play remains two hours in this checked version; all displayed figures bind to results.

The published view's thesis and `play.why` include old typed financial figures. They are not copied. fi-00 reuses the current market card's investment-summary fields, including its return basis and secured-share denominator. The briefing's older operating-model return and the card's investment return must not be swapped. The live action still refers to an autumn priority bill; fi-12 gives the ministry's newer timetable. Both issues are flagged for the analyst, with no model or view-store edits.

**Competing explanation:** lower reserve prices may reflect cross-border competition and procurement moving between annual and hourly channels as well as batteries. Growing restoration needs, industrial flexibility and energy trading can absorb some battery capability. A connected-fleet comparison alone cannot isolate the cause of prices or saturate every service.

**Disconfirming condition:** qualified domestic supply available to the relevant product could become tight relative to procurement, or realised after-cost trading and signed floor terms could differ materially from the model. Either can change the investment meaning. A different live call, play or best duration requires re-sign-off; research does not silently re-grade it.

### Rules for every shot

- **Figures:** every page figure, date, tick, tooltip and accessibility value uses a named bound token. Financial tokens resolve to existing market-card/section fields; historical and observed-data tokens refer to the dated evidence records in §9. No literal number or generated financial sentence is copied from stale prose.
- **Controls:** only era, product, timescale, evidence drawer, teaching battery preset and hotspot. Preserve navigation, language, selection and evidence state through Back/refresh as in the WA plan. No scenario changes the investment case.
- **Evidence:** each shot has one marker using the site’s fixed strings, followed by “What would change it:” / “会改变判断的证据：”. The opening inherits its card’s marker. The signed-off strengths and evidence-change conditions are retained; authoring checks and workflow status stay off the page.
- **Time and rights:** observations solid, future notices/plans dashed, examples explicitly **Example / 示例**. Use original diagrams; link documents rather than redistributing their assets. Capacity-price and energy-price evidence never share an unlabelled axis.
- **Teaching state:** usable energy and headroom are qualitative unless a separately approved example supplies them. The starting duration is the play's bound duration. The display never certifies full-power participation from nominal MWh.
