# Ireland duration correction and investment learning — 1 November 2026

The analyst approved the six-hour candidate, infrastructure-fund **Wait** and a historical learning record on 1 November 2026. An independent read-only review then reproduced the cash flows. This record supersedes the earlier four-hour recommendation without deleting it from `view/changelog.jsonl`.

## What changed

The four-hour model used a 66% illustrative capacity credit with the 2029/30 price. The same auction's official 40 < IC ≤ 50 MW row instead gives 27.7%, 42.5% and 54.9% at four, six and eight hours. For the standardised 50 MW unit, multiply its initial capacity by the applicable factor; the rows are not progressive tax brackets. Actual project qualification and awarded MW remain authoritative.

At EUR135.50/kW of credited capacity/year, four-hour revenue is EUR37,533.50/MW of nameplate/year; six-hour revenue is EUR57,587.50. The auction took place on **26 March 2026**, with final results on **5 May**, correcting the earlier plan's April date. This is an awarded-vintage illustration, not a price still available to an unawarded entrant.

The EUR8,000/MW/year services seed and its existing decay remain approved assumptions. They represent uncontracted competitive income within trading; they do not extend DS3 tariff rights beyond 30 September 2027. Relative operating-year decay is a screening assumption, not a dated forecast of FASS commencement.

## Why new projects do not invalidate the screen

Public reporting confirms active development and construction, but does not disclose a comparable, complete 50 MW six-hour equity IRR. A project's original decision date, auction vintage, actual awarded MW, costs, co-location and offtake can differ from this reference. Planning, RTB acquisition, procurement and construction are distinct milestones.

Public system research uses lower future long-duration construction assumptions, sometimes with materially higher operating costs. It measures system benefits, which are not automatically project cash flows. The reference CAPEX and annual trading seed therefore remain research gates. A bankable case needs scope-complete construction and operating budgets plus hourly dispatch and revenue co-optimisation, net of charging, losses, optimiser fees and capacity obligations. No lower cost or higher revenue sensitivity has been adopted as a new base.

Before underwriting, verify award quantity, price, term, delivery start, transfer and reliability-option liabilities; reconcile construction/COD with the actual capacity payment calendar. The platform's 2.5% nominalisation and post-contract renewal are analyst assumptions, not verified 2029/30 contractual rights. Grid charging reform removes specified charging transmission charges, not every grid fee.
